# Unit Economics — Assumptions Only

## Mobile eSIM subscription scenario
Illustrative price $12.99 / active line / month; assumed wholesale $5.00; payment, support and platform variable costs $1.50. Contribution = $6.49 / line / month **before CAC, fixed overhead, taxes and compliance**. The $5.00 wholesale number is NOT a supplier quote.

| Active lines | Monthly revenue | Contribution before CAC/fixed costs |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | $1,299 | $649 |
| 1,000 | $12,990 | $6,490 |
| 5,000 | $64,950 | $32,450 |

## Metrics
- Gross margin by carrier and plan; contribution after support/payment/roaming.
- Activation success rate from mainland China.
- OTP delivery success by permitted service category (no universal guarantee).
- Refunds, chargebacks, fraud loss, carrier suspensions.
- CAC, churn, net revenue retention, LTV, payback period.
- Carrier minimum commitments, deposit and cash conversion cycle.

## Sensitivity
A $3 increase in wholesale cost reduces contribution from $6.49 to $3.49, a 46% decline. Pricing viability depends on real supplier rate cards and roaming behavior.
