# Compliance, Security and Abuse Controls

This is a research checklist, not legal advice. Obtain telecom and privacy counsel before launch.

- Carrier and MVNO/MVNE resale authorization; provider-of-record responsibilities.
- Customer identity checks, sanctions screening and lawful-use terms as required.
- US federal/state telecom requirements, taxes, emergency calling disclosures and number portability.
- US business SMS: applicable A2P 10DLC, consent, opt-out and carrier policies.
- China cross-border operations: local marketing, data protection, telecom and consumer rules.
- OTP confidentiality: customer-specific numbers only; no public/shared inbox, no OTP logging, no employee visibility by default.
- Clear disclaimers: third parties decide whether to send OTP; mobile numbers do not guarantee bank acceptance.
- Anti-fraud: per-customer number quotas, velocity checks, payments risk controls, carrier complaint response.
- Security: encryption, RBAC, audit logs, webhook signatures, secrets vault, data minimization, retention/deletion policy.
- Phone number ownership, renewal reminders, portability, account recovery and dispute processes.
